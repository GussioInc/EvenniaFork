MysticalMUD Help File Entries
(Combined)
This file combines the output of multiple 'help' commands captured from log files and
supplemented by the 'Mystical Help File Crawl' document. The text for each entry is
reproduced exactly as found in the logs where possible, otherwise sourced from the
document. City maps are included where available. Entries marked with [Review] may be
incomplete or require verification.

HELP AARAKOCRA
The Aarakocra are a race of intelligent, bird-like creatures that live in high, mountainous
regions. They have feathered wings, sharp talons, and powerful beaks. They are somewhat
xenophobic, tending to keep to their own kind. Because of their wings, they cannot wear body
armor, but are able to fly. Aarakocra have excellent eyesight, and are able to see in the dark.
They have a natural resistance to lightning based attacks.

HELP ACCURACY
Accuracy is a measure of how good you are at hitting things with physical attacks. It does not
affect spell attacks. It increases with level, and warriors gain more than other classes. It is also
affected by dexterity.

HELP ALIAS
Syntax: alias <new alias> <replacement commands> ​

alias <new alias> ​

alias

The alias command allows you to create shortened versions of commands, or string several
commands together into one new command. If you simply type 'alias', it will list all the aliases
you currently have defined. If you type alias <new alias>, it will show you the definition for that
alias.
To remove an alias, simply alias it to nothing. For example: 'alias n'.
You can use several special characters in your alias definitions: ^ represents a command
separator (like ; ) $1-$9 represents arguments given to the alias when used.
For Example: alias ks kill $1;smile $1;get corpse
This alias, when used as 'ks guard', would translate into: kill guard;smile guard;get corpse

HELP AMBUSH
Syntax: ambush <target>
This skill allows a thief or assassin to attempt to attack a target without initiating combat. If
successful, the thief/assassin will get a single attack round against the target, and then return
to hiding. The attack has a higher chance of success if the target is already engaged in
combat. If the attack fails, the thief/assassin may be revealed.
See also: HIDE, SNEAK

HELP ARENA
The Arena is a place where players can challenge each other to combat without fear of
permanent death. Within the Arena, combat is regulated, and special rules may apply.
To enter the arena, find the entrance usually located near the main town square. Once inside,
you can use the CHALLENGE command to initiate combat with another player present.
Losses in the arena do not typically result in experience loss or corpse retrieval issues, but
check the specific rules of the arena you are in. Wins and losses are often tracked on a
leaderboard (see AHALL).
See also: CHALLENGE, AHALL

HELP ARMOR CLASS
Armor Class (AC) is a measure of how well protected your character is against physical
attacks. A lower AC is better. Your AC is determined by the armor you wear, your dexterity,
your level, and certain spells or affects. Mobs also have AC. You can see your own AC with the
SCORE or ARMOR commands, and you can estimate a mob's AC using CONSIDER.
See also: SCORE, ARMOR, CONSIDER

HELP ARMOR TYPES
Different types of armor offer varying levels of protection (AC) and may have different effects
on your character. Armor types generally fall into these categories:
Cloth: Offers minimal protection. Leather: Light armor, offers some protection, good for
stealthy classes. Chain: Medium armor, offers good protection, may hinder stealth slightly.

Plate: Heavy armor, offers the best physical protection, but is heavy and ​
can
significantly hinder stealth and spellcasting for some classes. Shields: Worn in the off-hand,
adds significantly to AC.
Some armors may also be magical, providing additional bonuses beyond their base AC. The
type of armor your character can effectively use depends on their class and skills.
See also: ARMOR CLASS, EQUIPMENT

HELP BARD
Bard is a versatile class that blends the skills of a warrior, thief, and mage. Bards are masters
of performance, using music and song to create magical effects, inspire allies, and demoralize
foes. They are typically charismatic and knowledgeable, often serving as entertainers,
loremasters, and diplomats. While not as powerful in direct combat or spellcasting as
dedicated classes, their wide array of skills makes them adaptable to many situations.
See also: CLASSES, SPELLS, SKILLS, SONGS BARD

HELP BLINDFIGHT
Syntax: practice blindfight
Blindfight is a passive skill that allows a character to fight more effectively while blinded.
Characters without this skill suffer severe penalties to their accuracy and defense when they
cannot see their opponent. Blindfight mitigates these penalties, making the character less
vulnerable to blinding attacks or fighting in darkness.
See also: BLINDNESS, DARKNESS

HELP BLINDNESS
Syntax: cast 'blindness' <target>
Blindness is a detrimental spell used to magically obscure the target's vision. A blinded
character suffers significant penalties in combat unless they possess the Blindfight skill. The
effect typically lasts for a limited duration. It is a common offensive spell used by mages and
some clerics.
See also: BLINDFIGHT, CURE BLIND

HELP CHEATING DEATH
If you die in Mystical Mud you will suffer experience loss, and return to your recall point
(usually a temple in your hometown). All of your equipment will remain on your corpse where
you died. You must then run back and retrieve your corpse ('get corpse') before it decays
(corpses last about 3 real-life hours).
If you cannot retrieve your corpse, some temples offer services to summon it for a fee.
Alternatively, high-level clerics may be able to resurrect you at your corpse, potentially
mitigating some experience loss.
See also: DYING, CORPSE KEEPER, RECALL, RESURRECT

shared goals, hierarchies, and sometimes specialized benefits like clan halls, equipment, or

NEWBIE HELP - CLASS ABILITIES
In case you didn't already guess from your journey through the Hallway
of Learning, combat plays a large part in mudding. If you want to have
access to all the skills and spells that your class has to offer, you'll
need to gain experience in battle until you're capable of learning them.
To view what skills you will have available, type 'skills i', and use
'spells i' to see what spells your class will get. This will show a
listing of the skills/spells with the level you can start to practice
them at. Check out the help file for the 'skills' and 'spells' commands
for more uses.
The next thing you'll need to know is how to practice your skills
and spells. This is done at a guildmaster. All of the classes practice
at one of four guilds: Warrior, Thief, Cleric, and Mage. Go to the guild

which best suits your class. If you are not allowed in, go to another
guild to see if you are a member there. Once inside you can type
'practice' to see a list of the skills and spells you can improve,
and the number of practice sessions that you have left. If you see
something you want to practice, type 'practice (skill/spell)' and
your proficiency will increase slightly. Recommended skills/spells
to practice first are: (not all classes will have all the listed items)
WEAPON PROFICIENCY
Represented by 5 skills, slash, pierce, bludgeon, chop, and stab.
You can see which of these skills your current weapon uses by studying
combat text closely. A higher proficiency increases your chance to hit.
SPELLCASTING
The spellcasting skill will increase the rate at which you cast spells
and is greatly beneficial as your proficiency in this skill increases.
SPECIAL SKILLS/SPELLS
Take advantage of the strengths of your class. If you're a Priest,
get a healing spell early on to last longer before resting. If you're
a Wizard, the magic missile spell will greatly help you early on.
Swordsmen can get more attacks earlier then anyone else, and so on.
See ATTRIBUTES.

ARMOR CLASS
"AC" is your armor class, the lower it is the better protected you are.
It is a number from -2000 to 100, roughly defined as follows:
100 Butt naked.

0 Hm ... getting there.
-200 Well armored.
-650 About the best AC a player can have.
-865 Hades AC (so now you know).
-1000 A scary AC possessed by some truly nasty mobs.
-2000 You DON'T want to know who has this.
The status command displays your armor class as a number such as 78/10
this means 78 on a scale from 100 down (i.e., 7.8 on the 10-scale).
Basically, just ignore it and concentrate on the first number.

ATTRIBUTES
Within Mystical there are currently 6 base attributes. The maximum amount
which these attributes can reach is determines by your current race. They
will usually start at an average rating (approx. 9-13) and usually end at
the given attributes set for each race. These attributes can also be
modified by wearing certain types of equipment and/or casting certain
spells. The 6 base attributes are:
STR - A measure of how much brute force you have. It affects how
much DR (damroll) you have, and how much is added by STRADD. If you
have high strength (18 and above) and depending on the class you will get
a number after your strength. This is called STRADD, or additional
strength. This number will even further increase the damage you do with
a landed hit, though not by too much.
INT (intelligence) - Determines how smart your character is and how
quickly they can learn things. Higher intelligence also means you have
higher spellpower. Higher spellpower means doing more damage per cast.
WIS (wisdom) - A show of how wise your character is and thus how good
their judgement is. This comes into account when making decisions,
imparting information upon others, and many other tasks of everyday life.

High wisdom will result in your character having more mana and more
mana regeneration.
DEX (dexterity) - Determines how quickly your character can react and
and how agile they are in general. High dexterity will give you more movement points, and also increase your movement point regeneration.
CON (constitution) - This is how hardy your character is. It measures
how strong their frame is and how much physical punishment they can take.
A high constitution will give you access to higher hitpoints so you can
take more punishment.
CHA (charisma) - How good your character looks and how appealing they
are to others around them. A high charisma will give you access to higher
hitpoint regeneration.

NEWBIE HELP - DYING
DYING IS PERFECTLY NORMAL
For those of you out there who have never played a mud before and are
panicking just thinking about dying, calm down! It's a perfectly normal
occurrence and for some people happens multiple times each day! Dying
on Mystical, like on many other muds, doesn't cause severe problems for
your character. If all goes well, then all you lose is a large amount of
xp, and nothing else.
HOW YOU CAN DIE
There are two main ways you can die in Mystical. The first is death
in combat, which is the usual way. This of course happens when you
tackle a mob that's too tough for you (remember to always 'consider' new
mobs!), or you just get worn down by too many adversaries. The
second way to die is by walking into special rooms lovingly known as
death traps, or DT's for short. These rooms usually have warnings in
the descriptions of the surrounding rooms, and there are spells and
skills that'll help you detect them. Even so, they still claim lives.

WHAT HAPPENS WHEN YOU DIE
Well the first thing you'll notice is that you'll be dumped back into
the main menu, with a message displaying how much xp you lost. This
will likely be confusing for a while, but you should get used to it.
You can now re-enter Mystical in the same way you usually would. You'll
enter at the temple of your hometown with 1 Hp and the same amount of Mn
and Mv that you died with. Also any maintained spells will have been
dispelled.
If you died by walking into a DT, you'll find that your body has
retained all of it's possessions by the will of the immortals. However
if you died in combat then you have a tougher task ahead, for your
corpse along with most of your possessions lies out in the place where
you died! If you remember where your corpse is and think you can get it
safely, then go ahead and try, else you'll need to ask for a CR (corpse
retrieval). NEW! A Corpse Keeper has taken residence in New Thalos. You
may ask him to do the CR for you, though he may charge you a fair sum of
gold for his services.
ASKING FOR CR'S
A CR is simply asking someone to get your corpse if you can't find
the way to it, or you died in a really tough area. Just state on OOC
that you need a CR done, and most of the players will know what you're
talking about. If you're lucky, a sympathetic soul will heed your plea
and try to help you. Try to remember the area where you died as it can
greatly speed retrieval efforts. If another player finds your corpse,

they'll return it to you promptly and you can retrieve your belongings
by typing 'get all corpse'. Don't worry about others trying to loot
your corpse, it's quite impossible for anyone but yourself to loot your
own corpse.

ATTRIBUTES
Within Mystical there are currently 6 base attributes. The maximum amount
which these attributes can reach is determines by your current race. They
will usually start at an average rating (approx. 9-13) and usually end at
the given attributes set for each race. These attributes can also be
modified by wearing certain types of equipment and/or casting certain
spells. The 6 base attributes are:
STR - A measure of how much brute force you have. It affects how
much DR (damroll) you have, and how much is added by STRADD. If you
have high strength (18 and above) and depending on the class you will get
a number after your strength. This is called STRADD, or additional
strength. This number will even further increase the damage you do with
a landed hit, though not by too much.
INT (intelligence) - Determines how smart your character is and how
quickly they can learn things. Higher intelligence also means you have
higher spellpower. Higher spellpower means doing more damage per cast.
WIS (wisdom) - A show of how wise your character is and thus how good
their judgement is. This comes into account when making decisions,
imparting information upon others, and many other tasks of everyday life.
High wisdom will result in your character having more mana and more
mana regeneration.
DEX (dexterity) - Determines how quickly your character can react and
and how agile they are in general. High dexterity will give you more move-

ment points, and also increase your movement point regeneration.
CON (constitution) - This is how hardy your character is. It measures
how strong their frame is and how much physical punishment they can take.
A high constitution will give you access to higher hitpoints so you can
take more punishment.
CHA (charisma) - How good your character looks and how appealing they
are to others around them. A high charisma will give you access to higher
hitpoint regeneration.

The card system in Mystical consists of these commands:

To get the card game started you must be in a group, and
also in a card playing room, such as Komarg's Casino. The
leader of the group has access to the get, shuffle, and deal
commands.
As the dealer (leader of the group) you must know these commands:

get:
To get the deck of cards and start a game, be the leader
of a group and type "get cards". This gives you the
cards and lets you use the other commands the dealer needs.
shuffle:
To shuffle the cards type "shuffle cards".
deal:
To deal cards to group members type:
"deal <persons name> <amount of cards>"
Example: deal Marvin 2
To deal all players in the group the same amount of cards
you type "deal all <amount of cards>"
Example: deal all 5
look:
To look at the cards you are holding type "look cards"
When you look at your cards they have a position number.
They are numbered from left to right.
/Q c/ /8 s/ /7 d/ /5 c/ /3 h/
Pos 1 Pos 2 Pos 3 Pos 4 Pos 5
sort:
To have your hand automatically sorted from high to low type
"sort cards"
order:
To order your cards manually, type

"order cards <position 1> <position 2>"
Example: order cards 4 1
This will switch the card in position 4 to position 1.
drop:
To drop a card or cards you have to specify their position number.
"drop cards <position 1> <position 2>"
Example: drop cards 2 5
This will drop the cards in the 2nd and 5th positions.
show:
Show displays the cards you have to everyone in the room.
"show cards"

NEWBIE HELP - DYING
DYING IS PERFECTLY NORMAL
For those of you out there who have never played a mud before and are
panicking just thinking about dying, calm down! It's a perfectly normal
occurrence and for some people happens multiple times each day! Dying
on Mystical, like on many other muds, doesn't cause severe problems for
your character. If all goes well, then all you lose is a large amount of
xp, and nothing else.
HOW YOU CAN DIE
There are two main ways you can die in Mystical. The first is death
in combat, which is the usual way. This of course happens when you
tackle a mob that's too tough for you (remember to always 'consider' new
mobs!), or you just get worn down by too many adversaries. The
second way to die is by walking into special rooms lovingly known as
death traps, or DT's for short. These rooms usually have warnings in
the descriptions of the surrounding rooms, and there are spells and
skills that'll help you detect them. Even so, they still claim lives.
WHAT HAPPENS WHEN YOU DIE
Well the first thing you'll notice is that you'll be dumped back into
the main menu, with a message displaying how much xp you lost. This
will likely be confusing for a while, but you should get used to it.
You can now re-enter Mystical in the same way you usually would. You'll

enter at the temple of your hometown with 1 Hp and the same amount of Mn
and Mv that you died with. Also any maintained spells will have been
dispelled.
If you died by walking into a DT, you'll find that your body has
retained all of it's possessions by the will of the immortals. However
if you died in combat then you have a tougher task ahead, for your
corpse along with most of your possessions lies out in the place where
you died! If you remember where your corpse is and think you can get it
safely, then go ahead and try, else you'll need to ask for a CR (corpse
retrieval). NEW! A Corpse Keeper has taken residence in New Thalos. You
may ask him to do the CR for you, though he may charge you a fair sum of
gold for his services.
ASKING FOR CR'S
A CR is simply asking someone to get your corpse if you can't find
the way to it, or you died in a really tough area. Just state on OOC
that you need a CR done, and most of the players will know what you're
talking about. If you're lucky, a sympathetic soul will heed your plea
and try to help you. Try to remember the area where you died as it can
greatly speed retrieval efforts. If another player finds your corpse,
they'll return it to you promptly and you can retrieve your belongings
by typing 'get all corpse'. Don't worry about others trying to loot

HOLY WORDS
Holy words are the most potent of all spells, transforming long periods of
tedious memorization into a single, sublime burst of arcane majesty. They can
only be learned by the most dedicated spellcasters, but they have truly
devastating effects. No saves are allowed against holy words of any kind, nor
is there a way to resist them.

PREPARING
A holy word must be memorized before you will be able to use it. This is done
by casting the spell as normal. This can be a long and tedious process in many
cases. When it reaches 100%, you will have memorized it.
All holy words you have memorized will show up in your statistics window. You
can't have more than one of any given holy word memorized simultaneously. i.e.
You may have a 'Reckoning' and a 'Restore' memorized, but not two of 'Restore'.
USING
Holy words may only be used in combat, and some have additional restrictions
outlined under their individual descriptions. You use a memorized holy word
by typing, not saying, it. e.x.:
reckoning
NOT
'reckoning'
Using aliases is recommended for the longer ones. Once you use the word in
combat, your memory will be cleared of that word, and you will need to memorize
it again.
