ROGUE 

Rogues are shady types that specialize and excel in equally shady ordeals.
While having no grasp on the arcane or other magical abilities, they are one
of the few classes that can steal, and focus around backstabbing and circling.
They tend to be a bit more specialized than thieves, but can not be used in
multi-classing. They're also adept at sneaking and hiding, and they tend not
to fatigue as quickly as other classes. 
   
Armor Classification: Leather

Drow, Dwarf, Flind, Giant, Giff, Githzerai, Gnoll, Gnome, Halfling, Half-orc,
