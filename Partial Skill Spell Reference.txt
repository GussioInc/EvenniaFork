ARCHERY 

Archery is a ranger skill that allows rangers to use ranged weapons
in battle.  In order to use archery, the ranger must wield a fireweapon
and hold a projectile in his offhand.  The ranger uses the shoot 
command to fire his weapon at his opponent.  The ranger can have range 
up to eight rooms away.  Damage is based on dexterity.

ASSASSINATE 

Assassinate is a way to sneak up on a person and attempt to place your dagger
in his back, at exactly the point where it does most damage.  A successful
attempt will halve your opponent's health instantly. A good begin is half the
work done!
The chance of successfully assassinating a person depends on how well you
have practiced assassinate at your guild.

BACKSTAB 


Rogue class' only
Backstab is a way to sneak up on a target and attempt to place your weapon
in its back at exactly the weakest spot to inflict maximum damage. A success-
ful backstab gives a damage multiplier that is level based. The chance of
successfully backstabbing something depends on how well you have practiced
backstab and your attributes. You can always backstab a sleeping target.


 BARRAGE

BASH 

Fighters only

The success of a bash depends upon how well you are learned. To bash someone
is to attempt to 'knock' that person over. If you succeed you will inflict
10 points of damage, and cause the victim to fall on its rear. The victim 
will be unable to issue any commands for 4 rounds of violence. If you miss 
you lose your balance and fall.

A bash makes you unable to type commands for 4 rounds of violence.
Note that hitting a person who is resting/sitting will inflict 1.5*damage.

 BREAK 

One of the core Assassin skills, break allows the assassin
to set up his prey before going in for the kill.

To use: break <option> <target>

Assassins have the option to break three of their target's
abilities:

ARMOR - break armor <target>
 <upon use> no effect

POISON - break poison <target>
 <upon use> reduces target's poison resistance by 2/3

NERVE - break nerve <target>
 <upon use> reduces target's total hit point regeneration
 to 0 and total move regeneration by 1/2

If the target with the break flees or is circled or
backstabbed, the breaks will activate and deal 1/8
of the target's maximum hit points in damage, and
then the breaks will disappear. Break Armor, however
applies a debuff that reduces the target's armor
by the assassin's dexterity, lasting 1 minute.

However, if combat is ended in any way besides the
target fleeing (eg. via summoning, the assassin flees,
etc.), then the breaks will remain until the target
flees manually or is backstabbed or circled.

Using the break command delays the user for 2 rounds
regardless of success and is not aggressive.

Other classes that can backstab or circle may also
activate an assassin's breaks if they use those skills
on a target that has breaks applied on them.

See also: ASSASSIN

DIRE BEAR FORM 

Dire bear form is a druid spell that allows the druid to shapeshift
into a bear to take on qualities of a warrior class.  In this form,
the druid's int and wis become his str and dex respectively. The druid
also gets an added large bonus to his con and small bonus to his hitroll.
Use maintain 'dire bear form' to enter this form and use 
cancel 'dire bear form' to return to your caster form.  You can switch
into the dire wolf form from this form at any time.  You cannot cancel
back to caster form in battle.

 DIRE STRIKE 

Dire strike is a druid skill used to stun an opponent that requires the
druid to be in dire bear form or dire wolf form.  In dire bear form,
the stun lasts for eight rounds.  In dire wolf form, the stun lasts for
six rounds.  The damage done is equal to 25 * number of rounds of stun. 

 
DIRE WOLF FORM 

Dire wolf form is a druid spell that allows the druid to shapeshift
into a wolf to take on qualities of a thief class.  In this form,
the druid's int and wis become his dex and str respectively. The druid
also gets an added large bonus to his hitroll and small bonus to his con.
Use maintain 'dire wolf form' to enter this form and use 
cancel 'dire wolf form' to return to your caster form.  You can switch
into the dire bear form from this form at any time.  You cannot cancel
back to caster form in battle.

HOLY WORDS 

Holy words are the most potent of all spells, transforming long periods of
tedious memorization into a single, sublime burst of arcane majesty.  They can
only be learned by the most dedicated spellcasters, but they have truly
devastating effects. No saves are allowed against holy words of any kind, nor
is there a way to resist them.
PREPARING:

A holy word must be memorized before you will be able to use it.  This is done
by casting the spell as normal.  This can be a long and tedious process in many
cases.  When it reaches 100%, you will have memorized it.
All holy words you have memorized will show up in your statistics window.  You
can't have more than one of any given holy word memorized simultaneously. i.e.
You may have a 'Reckoning' and a 'Restore' memorized, but not two of 'Restore'.

USING:
Holy words may only be used in combat, and some have additional restrictions
outlined under their individual descriptions.  You use a memorized holy word
by typing, not saying, it.  e.x.:
> reckoning
NOT
> 'reckoning'
Using aliases is recommended for the longer ones.  Once you use the word in
combat, your memory will be cleared of that word, and you will need to memorize
it again.
